To work out your exact age, count the **whole years** since your birth date, then the **whole months** since your last birthday, then the **days** left over. The [age calculator](/age-calculator/) does this instantly, but here is how to do it by hand.

## Worked example

Born **May 20, 1990**, age on **November 15, 2026**:

| Step | From | To | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whole years | May 20, 1990 | May 20, 2026 | 36 years |
| Whole months | May 20, 2026 | October 20, 2026 | 5 months |
| Remaining days | October 20, 2026 | November 15, 2026 | 26 days |

So the exact age is **36 years, 5 months and 26 days**.

## Step by step

1. **Years:** subtract the birth year from the current year. If this year's birthday hasn't happened yet, subtract one.
2. **Months:** count the whole months from your most recent birthday to today.
3. **Days:** count the days from that month mark to today.

## Total days lived

To get your age in days, count every day between your birth date and today, including leap days. The [date duration calculator](/date-duration-calculator/) gives this number along with weeks and months.

## Leap-day birthdays

If you were born on February 29, your birthday only appears every four years (with some century exceptions). In common years, many people — and this site's calculator — treat **February 28** as the birthday. Some people celebrate on March 1 instead, and legal rules differ by country.

## FAQ

### Why do online age calculators sometimes disagree by a day?

They may count month ends differently (for example, from January 31 to the end of February). Counting whole months first, then days, avoids most of these differences.

### Does the time of day I was born matter?

Only if you want your age in hours. For years, months and days, the date is enough.
