A **calendar day** is every day on the calendar, weekends and holidays included. A **business day** is a weekday (Monday to Friday) that is not a public holiday. The same "10 days" can land on two very different dates depending on which one a contract, store or government office means.

## Worked example

Say today is **Monday, November 16, 2026** and you are told something will happen "in 10 days".

| Count | Calendar days | Business days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 days | Saturday, November 21, 2026 | Monday, November 23, 2026 |
| 10 days | Thursday, November 26, 2026 | Monday, November 30, 2026 |
| 10 days, skipping US Thanksgiving (Nov 26) | — | Tuesday, December 1, 2026 |

Ten calendar days lands on Thanksgiving itself. Ten business days skips two weekends and lands four days later — five days later if the Thanksgiving holiday is also skipped.

## How to count calendar days

1. Treat today as day 0.
2. Count forward one day at a time, including Saturdays and Sundays.
3. Watch the month length: 28, 29, 30 or 31 days.

## How to count business days

1. Treat today as day 0, even if today is a weekday.
2. Count forward, skipping every Saturday and Sunday.
3. If your deadline excludes public holidays, skip those too. Which holidays count depends on the country (and sometimes the state or province).

A quick shortcut: every full week adds 5 business days. So 10 business days is two full working weeks, and 20 business days is four.

## When each one is used

- **Shipping and bank transfers** usually quote business days ("arrives in 3–5 business days").
- **Return windows, notice periods and many legal deadlines** usually use calendar days.
- **Contracts** should say which they mean. If one doesn't, ask — do not assume.

Always read the exact wording of the document you are dealing with. When it says "working days", it means business days.

## Common mistakes

- **Counting today as day 1.** Most rules count from the next day.
- **Forgetting holidays.** A 5-business-day turnaround that spans Christmas can take much longer than a normal week.
- **Mixing countries.** UK bank holidays and US federal holidays are different lists.

## FAQ

### Is Saturday a business day?

Usually no. Business days are Monday to Friday, although some banks and shops open on Saturdays.

### How many business days are in a year?

A year has 260 or 261 weekdays (262 in some leap years). Subtract the public holidays that fall on weekdays where you are.

### Do business days include public holidays?

No. A public holiday is not a business day, even when it falls on a weekday.
